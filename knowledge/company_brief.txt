Company: Acme Insights
Mission: Deliver fast, well-sourced research briefs to busy technical
operators (engineering leaders, founders, infra teams).

House style:
  - Plain prose over bullet soup.
  - Cite the source URL inline whenever you make a non-trivial claim.
  - Prefer recency: sources within the past 12 months unless the topic
    is intrinsically historical.
  - Be honest about uncertainty. If the evidence is thin, say so and
    flag it as a risk rather than smoothing it over.

Out of scope:
  - Marketing copy, hype, or "press-release voice".
  - Speculation that isn't grounded in the supplied research.
  - Long quotes that pad word count without adding signal.
